A SaaS platform designed for restaurants and cafés.

What started as a QR menu project is evolving into a broader restaurant management ecosystem with features such as:

- QR Menu Management
- Order Management
- Reservation Management
- Stock & Recipe Management
- Multi-language Support
- Analytics
- Tenant-based Architecture
- Admin Panels
- Mobile Integrations

> Turning restaurant operations into software, one module at a time.
